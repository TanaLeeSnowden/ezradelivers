About Ezra Delivers

Marketing shouldn't be the reason your business stays small.

Most small business owners know exactly what they should be doing for marketing. They just don't have the hours, the budget for an agency, or the patience for one more piece of software.

Why we exist

Hiring a marketing agency can run $2,000–$5,000 a month. Doing it yourself is free — except for the evenings and weekends it quietly eats up. Ezra Delivers was built for the space in between: real marketing output, built around your actual business, without the retainer or the learning curve. Want just the words, or the whole thing finished — graphics included? That's your call, not ours.

We start with the most common bottleneck — showing up consistently on social media — and build outward from there, in the order small businesses actually need it: get seen, get found on Google, build trust through reviews, then stay in touch by email. Every product on our roadmap answers the question you'll naturally ask right after the one before it.

How we work

No dashboards to log into. No software to manage. You tell us about your business once, and the finished work lands in your inbox within about an hour — ready to use, always yours to edit.

We're a small, independent operation, not a venture-backed platform chasing a land grab. That means we build one thing at a time, get it right, and only move to the next product once the current one genuinely works for the business owners using it.

Meet Ezra

Ezra is the name behind everything we build for you. It means "helper, builder, restorer" — and that's the job: quietly building your marketing so you can get back to running your business.

When you email us, you're talking to real people — the Ezra Delivers team — who stand behind every order.

What Ezra is built on

Every pack is built specifically around the details you share about your business — not pulled from a generic template. Think of it less like software you have to operate, and more like a quiet, capable partner handling the part of your business you don't have time for.

What we believe

Generic, robotic-sounding content is easy to spot and easy to ignore. Everything we build starts with your actual business.

Done means done
